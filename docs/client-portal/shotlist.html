Photo Shot List

Ten photos from your phone, and the site fills itself in

You don't need a photographer. Your iPhone in daylight beats a studio shot that looks like a catalog. Shoot everything horizontal and vertical when you can, stand back a little farther than feels natural, and upload straight to the portal folder named for each shot — Files → 03 Your Photos. Upload originals; don't screenshot or text them to yourself first.

Three rules that make phone photos look expensive: turn off the flash and shoot near the front window · wipe the lens · hold the phone level (turn on the grid in Camera settings). Portrait mode is fine for jewelry, off for racks.
