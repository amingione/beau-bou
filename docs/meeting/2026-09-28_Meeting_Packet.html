PAGE 4 · HOW IT CONNECTS

Register, website, wholesale, rewards

Product sync (Step 3)

Enter a product once; it shows in Epos and on the website. Sizes, colors, brands and categories carry across. Customer details and order history sync too. We finish this before the launch date, then replace the sample products with your real ones.

Rewards (Step 4)

Shoppers earn points for every purchase, in store or online. Points turn into a discount on a later purchase. Balances show in their account on the website. We're building this on tools you already pay for — Epos, Shopify and Zapier — so there's no separate rewards-app subscription. The private connection between them is already installed on your store; the piece left is the points logic and your rules below.
