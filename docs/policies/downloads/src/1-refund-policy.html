We have a 7-day return policy, which means you have 7 days after receiving (or picking up) your item to request a return.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition you received it — unworn, unwashed, with tags attached — and you'll need your receipt or proof of purchase.

To start a return, bring your item and receipt to the shop at 264 W Marion Ave, Punta Gorda, FL 33950, or email us at beyoutifulboutiquebymichele@gmail.com and we'll send you a return address. Items sent back to us without first requesting a return will not be accepted.

You can always reach us with return questions at beyoutifulboutiquebymichele@gmail.com or (941) 369-1628.

Damages and issues
Please inspect your order as soon as you receive it and contact us right away if an item is defective, damaged, or wrong, so we can make it right.

Exceptions / non-returnable items
Final sale and clearance items cannot be returned or exchanged, along with swimwear and jewelry. Get in touch if you have questions about a specific item.

Exchanges
The fastest way to get what you want is to return the item you have and, once that return is accepted, place a separate order for the new item.

Refunds
We'll let you know once we've received and inspected your return and whether it's approved. Approved returns are issued as store credit; approved returns for a damaged or incorrect item may instead be refunded to your original payment method. Please allow a few business days for it to post.
