# Privacy Policy

Be-YOU-tiful Boutique by Michele ("we") operates this store on Shopify. This policy explains what we collect and why.

**What we collect.** When you shop or sign up, we collect the details needed to fulfill your order and stay in touch: name, email, phone, shipping and billing address, and order history. Payment details are processed by Shopify Payments and its partners — we never see or store your full card number. Our site uses cookies for the cart, checkout, and basic analytics.

**How we use it.** To process and deliver orders, arrange in-store pickup, answer your messages, and — only if you opt in — send occasional emails about new arrivals and events. Unsubscribe any time from the link in any email.

**Who we share it with.** Shopify (our store platform), payment processors, and shipping carriers, each only to the extent needed to complete your order. We don't sell your information.

**Your choices.** Email us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Florida residents and others may have additional rights under applicable law; we honor them.
