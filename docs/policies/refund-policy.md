# Shipping & Returns

## Shipping
Orders ship within 1–2 business days from Punta Gorda, Florida by USPS. Most Florida addresses arrive in 2–3 days; the rest of the US in 3–5. You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves the shop. Rates are shown at checkout; orders over the free-shipping threshold ship free.

## In-store pickup
Choose **Pickup** at checkout — no charge. We'll email or text when your order is ready, usually the same business day. Bring your order number or the name on the order to 264-A W Marion Ave. Pickups are held for 14 days.

## Returns & exchanges
Unworn, unwashed items with tags attached may be returned within **14 days** of delivery (or pickup) for store credit, or exchanged for another size or style. Return in store with your order confirmation, or email us for a return address — return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

**Final sale:** swimwear, jewelry, and all sale items cannot be returned or exchanged.

## Damaged or wrong item
Email or message us within 3 days of delivery with a photo and your order number and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund, shipping included.
