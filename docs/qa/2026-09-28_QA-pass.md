## Accessibility basics — issues found
1. **Homepage `<h1>` is empty** (no text content). Every page should have one meaningful, visible H1 — this also weakens the homepage's SEO signal.
2. **8 of 26 homepage images have no `alt` text**, and none are decorative: the 4 "Shop Styles" look-grid thumbnails (`ss-tile__img`), 2 category tiles, and the "Visit Our Store" storefront photo, plus one more.
3. **Top promo banner contrast fails WCAG AA**: "GET 20% OFF YOUR FIRST ONLINE ORDER!" renders in white text at 75% opacity over the teal band (`rgb(91,147,160)`), computed contrast ≈3.4:1 (need 4.5:1 for normal-size text). Fix: remove the opacity/use solid white, or darken the teal for that band.
4. Good: form labels are properly associated (verified on the Comment field), the icon-only newsletter submit button has an accessible "Subscribe" name, look-tile links have descriptive names ("Shop the look: Blue Tonic"), and button text contrast passes (5.4:1).

## SEO metadata — not in place yet
1. **Homepage has no meta description at all** — the single most important page for a search snippet.
2. Contact page and the Dresses collection page (spot-checked) also have no meta description.
3. About page's meta description still says "sizes XS to 3X" — should be "XXS to 3X" per the contract; the site-wide XXS fix recorded earlier didn't reach this field.
4. `og:image` is served over `http://` (not `https://`) site-wide — should be forced to https for social previews.
5. Product pages do have per-product meta descriptions and JSON-LD (`Organization` + `ProductGroup`) — solid foundation, but the 18 `TEST —` products' descriptions currently lead with "Sample product for website testing only" (expected, resolves when real products replace them).
6. robots.txt and sitemap.xml are present and correctly Shopify-generated — no action needed, but moot until the storefront password comes off (nothing is crawlable while gated).
